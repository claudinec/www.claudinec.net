I'm having a gloomy day (lifelong depression plus lifelong pessimism plus sensitivity to injustice) so I'm making a reminder to myself of the little things I can do to feel less helpless.

- Low effort
  - Clean up vandalism, spam, [disinformation, and abuse on Wikipedia](https://www.citationneeded.news/elon-musk-and-the-rights-war-on-wikipedia/)
  - If I find an interesting article while doomscrolling, pause to add it to my bookmarks and [ArchiveBox]({{< ref "/tags/archivebox" >}}) to read later when my mind is clearer
- Medium effort
  - Pick up straightforward archiving and cataloguing tasks posted to the [Safeguarding Research forum](https://safeguarding-research.discourse.group/)
  - Add documentation on archiving methods on the forum or on this blog
- High effort
  - Data archaeology – Excavating saved collections to compile user-friendly indexes
