I have published a [privacy policy]({{< ref "/privacy" >}}) covering this website and any interactions that may be initiated from here (e.g. links to my email address). Does a small personal IndieWeb site really need a privacy policy? I think it's useful for any creator to think about the assumptions they make about privacy and security when building their website. Writing it down can be a healthy way to examine one's values relating to technology (as it did for me). And a [thorough list of what private data you do or don't collect](https://indieweb.org/disclosure) can be a great illustration of why small indie websites *don't* need to invade their visitors' privacy.

I have also tried to think of readers who might not be familiar with web technologies, and linked to descriptions or definitions of terms like *IP address* and *cookie*. Do [get in touch]({{< ref "/hello" >}}) if you think there's anything here that could be explained better, or if I've made any mistakes.
