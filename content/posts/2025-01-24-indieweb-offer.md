[Molly White](https://www.mollywhite.net/micro/entry/202501190959):

> The TikTok ban, the Musk Twitter takeover, the Facebook moderation policy changes, the Republicans' rapidly intensifying crackdowns on speech... let these be the proof you needed to move anything you care about online to a space you control.

> Digital sovereignty is more important than ever.

I've been going on about this for years. Maybe this is the moment when *you* decide you want to take control of your online identity, but what if you don't know how to start?

[Get in touch](https://www.claudinec.net/hello/) – post to Bluesky or the fediverse and we can brainstorm together, or send me an email if you want to keep it private. What's keeping you from getting your own domain or starting a blog? The answers and solutions may be different for everyone, but these are not new questions.

I used to help build websites using complex content management systems (Drupal and WordPress) before I burnt out. More recently, there's been a [renaissance of personal, creative, and weird websites](https://www.claudinec.net/posts/2024-05-26-indieweb/) – digital sovereignty has never been exclusively for large businesses or organisations. And there are so many more options now in the middle ground between *huge database-driven content management system* and *individually hand-coded HTML files*. I'm not going to build a whole website for you but may I can help you learn how to build one yourself.
